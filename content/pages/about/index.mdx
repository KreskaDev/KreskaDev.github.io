**Senior .NET architect. Builds agentic systems, writes about reasoning.**

I am a senior .NET architect based in Białystok, Poland. Fifteen years of building distributed systems for clients in Poland, the UK, Sweden, the Netherlands, and the US: greenfield rewrites of legacy stacks, performance work at user-scale, and the architect-side outputs (ADRs, C4 diagrams, phase roadmaps) that keep a project coherent as the code grows. Since 2025 the hands-on work has shifted toward agentic systems: MCP servers, embedding-based pipelines, and the engineering practice around LLMs.

This blog is where I document that work. Some posts are engineering case studies: architecture write-ups of real systems, including the parts I would change. Others are first-principles analyses where the math earns its keep: Bayesian updates, hypothesis testing, model behavior under adversarial conditions. The blog itself is the other half of that documentation, built on Next.js, MDX, and Tailwind, hosted on GitHub Pages at zero cost, treated as a continuous study of pragmatic tradeoffs. I also keep a research curiosity in offensive security, which shows up here when it overlaps with the systems I work on.
